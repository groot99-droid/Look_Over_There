# The sports courts (`sports`), dressed

The owner's request: "Along side the playground create a basketball court, a tennis court, and a racetrack". The two courts are this scene, beside the playground and reached through a new gap in its south hedge; the racetrack is its own scene (`racetrack`, `docs/renders/racetrack/`), through this scene's south gap. Laid out first in the greybox (`tools/blender/build_overworld_greybox.py`, `sports()`), then dressed in Blender with the playground's method and look, and given an ambient-occlusion bake. Tone: liminal, the Overworld's bright plastic park look, empty and seemingly normal: two courts nobody plays on, the floodlights off, two balls and a tennis ball left out.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `world/sports.glb` | the art, art only: the static art merged by material, and the three balls as their own nodes |
| `world/sports.json` | the layout, written by `tools/blender/export_scene.py` from the `.blend`; equal (parsed) to `build_overworld_greybox.py --preview`'s |
| `world/lightmaps/sports_0.webp`, `_1.webp` | the AO bake (second UV set, `TEXCOORD_1`, linear): the ground, the courts and the hedges (2048), everything standing (2048) |
| `blender/scenes/sports.blend` | the saved scene (Git LFS): `Art`, `Play` (the balls), `Layout` (the `COL_` boxes and empties), `Export` (merged by material, with the AO UVs) |
| `blender/textures/overworld_sports/` | the textures the courts and the racetrack add, generated by the build (own work: acrylic, asphalt, concrete, shutter, chain-link and netting with alpha) |

| `docs/renders/sports/` | this file, the three review stills, `bake.json` (the bake's own report) and `brightness.txt` |

## The layout

60 m by 48 m inside its hedge (x -30 to 30, z -24 to 24, floor y 0), a 3 m path down the middle from the north gap to the south gap.

- **Tennis** (west, centre x -14): one doubles court, 23.77 m by 10.97 m (singles 8.23 m), the long axis north to south, on a 18.29 m by 36.58 m green surround (the run-off) inside a 3.6 m chain-link fence. The gate is in the fence's east side (z 4.7 to 6.3, 2.3 m high, a TENNIS sign over it on both faces). Net posts 0.914 m outside the doubles sidelines; the net (collider 1.07 m) sags to 0.914 m in the middle. The umpire's chair (2.6 m) stands at the east post, a bench either side of it.
- **Basketball** (east, centre x 14): a full court, 28 m by 15 m (FIBA), on a 2 m run-off, behind a 1 m green railing with a 2 m gate in its west side (z -10 to -8, a BASKETBALL sign both faces). Lines to FIBA: the keys (4.9 m by 5.8 m), free-throw circles (1.8 m), the three-point line (6.75 m, its straights 0.9 m in from the sidelines), restricted areas (1.25 m), the centre circle. Two hoops: a padded pole 1.1 m behind each baseline, an arm to a 1.8 m by 1.05 m backboard (its face 1.2 m in from the baseline, bottom at 2.9 m), the ring at 3.05 m with a net. Two benches by the west side of the halfway line.
- **Along the path:** four benches, a bin by each, three lamp posts; six floodlight masts (9 m: four at the tennis fence's corners, two east of the basketball court), their heads `Lamp_Emit`.
- **Hedges** round the edge, with an arch over each gap: the north gap's sign reads PLAYGROUND facing in (SPORTS facing out), the south gap's RACETRACK (SPORTS facing out). Two `Spot_groundskeeper`.

Links (`Link_` / `Arrive_`): `playground` at (0, 0, -24.6), arriving at (0, 0, -21.4) facing south; `racetrack` at (0, 0, 24.6), arriving at (0, 0, 21.4) facing north. The other sides: `playground.sports` (its south gap) and `racetrack.sports` (its north gap). Spots: `Spot_minigame_basketball` (the free-throw line of the north half, props equipment, parts, pays, what: "shoot hoops: free throws against the clock"), `Spot_minigame_tennis` (behind the south baseline: "serve and rally over the net"), two groundskeepers. Corridors gated by `--check`: the path, both gaps, both court gates.

Every solid has its collider in the layout (49): the fence runs (the gate's head is its own piece above 2.3 m), the net and its posts, the umpire chair, the benches, the bins, the poles, the masts, the lamp posts, the hedges. Everything else is `decor_`: the surfaces and lines, the hoops' arms, boards, rings and nets (all above head height), the floodlight heads, the signs.

## The balls

Each is its own node in `world/sports.glb`, named as in the greybox, its origin on its centre (the node's translation, in game coordinates) and its custom properties as glTF `extras`: `equipment`, `part`, `axis` "xyz", `motion` "physics", `range_deg` 0, `radius_m`, `what`, `pivot`. Materials of their own (`Play_Orange`, `Play_Black`, `Play_Tennis`), not baked. The greybox's `--check` gate "balls" reads them from this GLB.

## Light: ambient occlusion only

As the playground (`docs/BLENDER.md` R1, AO-only scenes): the baked materials carry `aomap` (`world/lightmaps/sports_<n>.webp`) and `aomap_intensity` 1.0, on the second UV set, and no `lightmap`; the page lights them with its sun and sky. `Lamp_Emit` (lamp and floodlight heads, strength 3) is switched by the page. Not baked: the lamp heads, the painted lines, the balls, and the three alpha-masked materials, `Chainlink`, `Net_Tennis` and `Net_Hoop` (glTF `alphaMode` MASK, cutoff 0.5, double-sided; hidden from the bake, so they cast no AO). What lies under the courts and the path is cut out of the ground and the surrounds, so it takes no texels.

Baked in Cycles on the rented GPU (`--samples 256`), OIDN-denoised, 8 px margin, linear (`bake.json`):

| Map | Size | What | AO distance | px/m | AO p10 / p50 / p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sports_0` | 2048 | the grass, the path, the courts and their surrounds, the hedges | 1.5 m | 23.8 | 0.33 / 0.99 / 1.00 |
| `sports_1` | 2048 | everything standing: fences, posts, masts, chair, benches, bins, hoops, signs, arches | 0.8 m | 16.8 | 0.03 / 0.75 / 1.00 |
