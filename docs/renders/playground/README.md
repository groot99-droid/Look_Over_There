# The playground (`playground`), dressed

The Overworld's playground (`docs/BLENDER_AREAS.md` section 4: "Real equipment, each a mini-game: swing, slide, seesaw, merry-go-round, monkey bars, sandbox, spring riders, climbing frame. Each piece gets clear ground around it. The moving parts are separate objects with their pivots set, named `Play_<equipment>_<part>`, so the page can move them"), dressed in Blender on the greybox's layout and given an ambient-occlusion bake. Tone: dreamcore, too bright: a plastic, pastel park that is empty and seemingly normal.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `world/playground.glb` | the art, art only: the static art merged by material, and the ten moving parts as their own nodes |
| `world/playground.json` | the layout, written by `tools/blender/export_scene.py` from the `.blend`; equal to `build_overworld_greybox.py --preview`'s (see "The layout") |
| `world/lightmaps/playground_0.webp`, `_1.webp` | the AO bake (second UV set, `TEXCOORD_1`, linear): the ground and the hedges (2048), everything standing (2048) |
| `blender/scenes/playground.blend` | the saved scene (Git LFS): `Art` (the pieces, one per greybox box), `Play` (the moving parts), `Layout` (the `COL_` boxes and empties), `Export` (merged by material, with the AO UVs) |
| `blender/textures/overworld_play/` | the textures the playground and the market share, generated by the build (own work: grass, path, paving, wood, hedge, plaster, the sign atlas) |
| `tools/blender/build_overworld_play.py` | the build for the playground and the market; it imports the greybox model, so no coordinate of the layout is typed in |

## What is in it

- **The ground:** a sunny lawn (generated grass texture, tinted), the sandy path east to west, a red rubber safety pad under each piece, sand in the sandbox.
- **The hedges** round the edge, with a round-edged top; an arch over each gap (east to the lot, west to the tree house) with a PLAYGROUND sign on both faces. The arch's posts stand inside the hedge.
- **The eight pieces**, built on the greybox's boxes: round steel posts and bars wherever the greybox has a thin box (the swing frame, the monkey bars' rails and uprights, the climbing frame, the lamp posts, the kiosk-style rails), plastic plates with softened edges, a guard rail of three bars on the slide's platform, yellow side rails down the chute, treads on the ladders and steps.
- **The moving parts** (table below): rubber swing seats on round chains, the seesaw's plank and handles, the merry-go-round as a round deck with a red top ring, its handrails and a ring rail, the spring riders as a horse, a duck and a bee (body, head, handle, seat) on a steel coil spring, the sandbox digger's seat and arm, the climbing frame's ship's wheel.
- **Along the path:** a low white picket fence each side (gates opposite each piece and the facades), four slatted benches with steel legs, a green bin at the end of each bench, four lamp posts with round lamp heads (`Lamp_Emit`), hopscotch and a painted line of dots on the path.
- **On the lawn:** six round flower beds with white stone rims, flowers along the hedges, a red ball and a blue ball left out, a bucket in the sandbox.
- **The two "more to come" facades** against the north hedge: plaster fronts with a fake door, two windows and a MORE TO COME sign.

Everything that stands has its collider in the layout; everything added that the greybox did not have is `decor_` (flowers, beds, paint, toys, the chute's side rails, the lamp heads), except the arches, whose posts stand inside the hedge's collider and whose beam and sign are above head height.

## The moving parts

Each is its own node in `world/playground.glb`, named as in the greybox, with its origin on its pivot (the node's translation is the pivot, in game coordinates) and its custom properties as glTF `extras`: `equipment`, `part`, `axis`, `motion`, `range_deg`, `pivot`, and `length_m`, `radius_m`, `what` or `parent` where the greybox gives them. They have materials of their own (`Play_*`, plain colours, no AO map) because they move. The greybox's `--check` gate "moving parts" reads them from this GLB.

## Light: ambient occlusion only

As the other outdoor scenes (the street, the meadow, the forest; `docs/BLENDER.md` R1, AO-only scenes): the baked materials carry `aomap` (`world/lightmaps/playground_<n>.webp`) and `aomap_intensity` 1.0, on the second UV set, and no `lightmap`; the page keeps them lit by its sun and sky and uses the bake as their ambient occlusion. No day or night set. `Lamp_Emit` (the lamp heads) is exported at strength 3, as part 1's lamps, and switched by the page. Not baked: the lamp heads, the painted lines (narrower than a texel), the flowers and goods (`Goods_*`), and the moving parts (`Play_*`).

Baked in Cycles on the rented GPU (`--samples 256`), Cycles' AO bake, OIDN-denoised, 8 px margin, written linear (`bake.json`, the bake's own report):

| Map | Size | What | AO distance | px/m | AO p10 / p50 / p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `playground_0` | 2048 | the lawn and its skirt, the path, the pads, the sand, the soil of the beds, the hedges | 1.5 m | 16.9 | 0.04 / 1.00 / 1.00 |
| `playground_1` | 2048 | everything standing: equipment, fence, benches, bins, lamps' posts, facades, signs, arches | 0.8 m | 19.1 | 0.04 / 0.56 / 1.00 |

The undersides of boxes standing on the ground are dropped before the bake (never seen).

## The layout

`world/playground.json` is written by `export_scene.py` from the `.blend` and is equal (parsed) to `build_overworld_greybox.py --preview`'s. It changed from the greybox's on purpose, and only by additions: the dressing's low fence (`Fence_N_1` to `Fence_N_6`, `Fence_S_1` to `Fence_S_6`: 0.8 m high, 0.1 m thick, along z = +-5.6 from x -44 to 44, with 3 m gates opposite each piece at x -36, -12, 12, 36 and the facades at x 0) and four bins (`Bin_1` to `Bin_4`, 0.5 x 0.9 x 0.5 m, at the west end of each bench). They are solid things you cannot walk through, and every visible solid needs its `COL_` box (`docs/BLENDER_AREAS.md`, the collision rules), so they are added in the greybox script (`FENCE_*`, `fence_runs()`, `playground()`) and the layout re-exported. Nothing that was there moved: colliders, links, spots, the corridors and every piece's clear ground are as before; the greybox's gates still pass (the fence keeps out of every clear ground and the path's corridor). 107 colliders (91 before), 2 links, 14 spots.
