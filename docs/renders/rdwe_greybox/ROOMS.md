# Right Door, Wrong Exit: the proposed new rooms (Phase C)

One line a room, in list order. Each has a minimal greybox (a shell, its doors and links, one or two key boxes for its trick, and the trick's Spot_), none dressed. The owner picks which rooms to dress. Super Liminal style, after docs/WORLD.md: object distortion and off-looking objects, perspective shifting, distorted halls, multi-choice navigation, psychological puzzles, environmental storytelling.
