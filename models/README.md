# models/

Optional 3D models for puzzle objects, loaded by `index.html` as `models/<id>.glb`. Each one replaces a placeholder drawn in code, so the game works without any of them.
Currently used: `gun.glb` (the ray gun), `cloak.glb`, `beacon.glb` and `fuse.glb`; `switch.glb` is looked for but not made yet. The fuse is the owner's Meshy model: a glass cartridge fuse, 2,096 triangles, 0.54 MB. Its 2048 px texture was re-saved as WebP with `tools/webp_glb.py` (geometry bytes unchanged), as for `items/`. See `docs/MODELS.md` for what to generate, the budget, and how to prepare the file.

`gun.glb` is the player's weapon, held in your hand and in teammates' (see `docs/GAMEPLAY.md`, "Fighting back"). It is Meshy's "Error Deletion Ray": an improvised ray gun (taped barrel, a cyan canister, an "ERR" display, a "404" label, an antenna and a glowing tip). The Meshy character-style export (16 MB, 10k triangles, three 2048 px PNG textures, a skeleton with no animations) was prepared with gltf-transform: skeleton removed (it is a static model), 1024 px WebP colour, normal and metal/rough maps, Draco, 0.88 MB, 10k triangles, one mesh. It is 3.2 units long (X, the muzzle points to -X), 1.7 high (the grip hangs below the barrel; Y starts at 0) and 0.9 wide, so it must be scaled to about 0.55 m long (about 0.17x) when it is held. An earlier, heavier export of the same gun (100k triangles) was replaced by this one.

`items/` holds the owner's 24 item models (the arsenal, consumables, dealer items, food and two extras), see `models/items/README.md`. In the game so far: the land mine and the nail-bomb tripwire (`trapMesh` in `index.html` loads `items/almond_water_land_mine` and `items/nail_bomb_tripwire` and hides the code-made shape behind them), and the five Going back in weapons held in your hands: the hunting crossbow (`items/hunting_crossbow`, 0.42 m, moved 5 cm left so its limbs clear the ammo lines), the pneumatic bolt driver (0.4 m), the stun baton (0.5 m), the moth jelly dart (0.5 m) and the propane flamethrower (0.55 m). Each is laid along the view by `heldModel` in `index.html`: the crossbow, driver, dart and flamethrower point along -X in their files (as the ray gun does) and the baton's prongs along +X. If a file cannot load, the code-made shape stays. The other 17 are not loaded yet.
